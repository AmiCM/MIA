# Most Loved Perfumes in 2010

Category: Fragrantica. Author: Elena Knezevic.

Fragrantica is thrilled to present to you the Fragrantica Choices from 2010. The prestigious FiFi Awards and your active discussion about them have induced us to look into our own statistics of the most-loved perfumes of our community. We collected and compared all your votes on the site to show you what our community likes.
