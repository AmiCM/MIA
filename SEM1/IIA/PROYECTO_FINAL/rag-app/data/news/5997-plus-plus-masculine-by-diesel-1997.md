Summary : Diesel’s third men’s fragrance is a sweet, juicy, creamy composition inspired by classic milk bottles and cartons with an unusual juicy topnote juxtaposed against a creamy milk note in the base. Perfumer : Unknown. Try this if you like: Juicy and fruity scents; creamy, vanillic basenotes; kitschy bottles and packaging. Pros & Cons : Love at first sniff for me due to the aforementioned sweet, juicy topnotes that remind me of Hawaiin Punch and that seem to last and last and last. I do get a milky note not unlike the soymilk note of Clinique’s Simply that I find intoxicating. With big sillage, excellent longevity, unique packaging and an incredible price, Plus Plus is perfect for gift giving. Notes : “Top notes are bergamot, orange, palm leaf and heliotrope; middle notes are jasmine, birch, sage, cardamom, coriander, lily-of-the-valley, violet and green notes; base notes are vetiver, musk, sandalwood, patchouli, oak moss, nutmeg, clove, cinnamon, tonka bean and anise.” Fragrantica.com Reminds me of : Dunhill Desire for Men , Sculpture pour Homme , Emporio Armani Diamonds for Men , Escada Tropical Punch , Polo Red , * Diesel Fuel for Life Men Designer’s Description : Discontinued; not available; but check out this brilliant chemical breakdown by Matt at Par-Fum.blogspot.com . Number of times tested : 100+ over the last 15 years. Number of sprays applied for this review : 2 sprays to the back of hand from a bottle I purchased. Fragrance strength : Eau de Toilette Development : ( Linear / Average / Complex) Diesel Plus Plus is like a well-blended cocktail; no one “flavor” really stands out and it’s similar from start to finish. Longevity : (Short / Average / Long-lasting ) I can get around 7-9 hours. Sillage : (A Little / Average / A Lot ) I’ve gotten a number of compliments when wearing this which tells me I’m not the only one who can smell it when I wear it. Note about the packaging : An opaque, white glass bottle with white rubber cap resembling an old-fashioned milk bottle is housed in a silver paper box resembling a milk carton. Did I mention the box is adorned with cows? Where can I buy it ? Found online for as little as $10 USD for a 2.5oz EDT spray. The Bottom Line : First there was Sculpture by Nikos . But after a friend adopted it as his own I quickly became disinterested as it always reminded me of him. When I discovered Diesel’s first men’s scent , the eponymous fragrance launched in the mid-90’s, I dismissed it as not my style. Then a few years later came Plus Plus and it couldn’t have been more different. Gone was the classical composition referencing men’s scents of times past and here was a surprisingly fruity, and milky (!), aroma that I had to sniff twice to make sure I hadn’t accidentally sampled the women’s version. Instant love! It immediately reminded me of Escada’s Tropical Punch fragrance or the top berry notes of Mugler’s Angel . In fact, Angel is a scent I come back to over and over again when considering Plus Plus. Remove the patchouli and chocolate from Angel, soften the caramel and vanilla and we’re not too far from Plus Plus . What really puzzles me are the reviews of this fragrance; they seem to be all over the place! Some folks describe sour milk while others go on and on about the cinnamon. All I can say is “what cinnamon?” And if this is what sour milk might smell like, I’d gladly keep a carton around as a room freshener, lol. If sweet and creamy interest you, like a fruity crème caramel, or if you are a bottle collector, or even if you are looking for an interesting youth-friendly and affordable fragrance, look no further than Diesel Plus Plus !
