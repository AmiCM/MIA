# Angel Muse Thierry Mugler: Skipping To The Dessert Menu

Category: Fragrance Reviews. Author: Elena Knezevic.

Angel Muse Thierry Mugler occupies the honorable first place in Fragrantica readers' preferences of perfumes launched in 2016 and was earlier reviewed by our writer Marlen Harrison HERE . It is interesting and encouraging when such contradictory fragrances appear in our end of year top fragrances list. Angel Muse is not just a simplified version of its celebrated original, as we have witnessed its flow to an easier and sweeter side in the new millennia editions. Angel Muse is a reinterpretation of the glorious original story. Olivier Polge has succeeded in delivering the new Chanel No 5 L'Eau , and I presume that young girls who chose this perfume may have a very blurry idea of how it all started; they do not need a bold historical reference to love it. It is interesting to see the progress of Angel Muse in the same direction, since Mugler really made a big effort to make a new star. We can see it in its design too, the new cosmic pebble from Mars is more than a re-using of the irregular angles the old perfume bottle; it is a great new bottle! If you find Angel Thierry Mugler memorable, in either an exciting or annoying way, the new Angel Muse has preserved the original Angel's capacity of evoking love/hate feelings. As the original Angel's charismatic patchouli purposely balances the sweet overload without any allusion to its masculinity (a Grand Dame, maybe even an old-lady, but never manly), the role of patchouly and vetiver in Angel Muse is defined in the press release as a "typically masculine" counterpart to its creamy sweetness. And that's a very accurate description of my perception of its first powerful blast! It points rather to the opposite sex. I easily get hung up on unexpected pairings and uneasy perfume openings, it captures my attention and makes me dwell upon its strange development; some consternation with a perfume is always preferable to absolute acceptance without any reaction. We keep considering the "disturbing" part with thoughts and associations until it becomes a pearl which makes the perfume special. In Angel Muse I find this disturbing part too disturbing without any obvious reason, except a quite random nod towards masculinity. You might reproach me for underestimating its advance message but all of what Angel had to say has been said very loudly, an olfactory shock, a punch has been already widely experienced and digested. "Angel Eau de Parfum is the star, while Angel MUSE orbits around the star" (Christophe de Lataillade, Creative Director for MUGLER fragrances) . Yes, it did not overcome its gravitation, while, for example, Chanel No 5 L'Eau did! Smelling Angel I am looking at an extraordinary woman supplying tons of exclusively feminine power, smelling Angel Muse I always catch myself searching for masculine features in her character. So why shouldn't I suggest this perfume to men? Its name and its provocative design (doesn't it look physiological to you?) might even contribute to its attractiveness to the opposite sex. At least, in this case we can talk about a refreshing offering, maybe not from Мars, but from very luxurious quarters on Earth. I am sure Angel Muse will add sparkle in the eye of men. I have great respect for the Thierry Mugler perfume brand for its playing big, only this time its message has missed me. I am skipping this sophisticated meal and call for a dessert! I can tell that the next Angel Muse is going to be a delightful Nutella. They should have started right from there.
