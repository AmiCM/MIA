# Bath & Body: ActivESSENCE Time-Released Mists with Skin Loving Extracts

Category: Bath & Body. Author: Dr. Marlen Elliot Harrison.

The last time I was in a Walgreens drug store I was curious to see what kind of fragrant products were on the shelves. Aside from the usual collection of brand name designer fragrances and bottom shelf bargains from Coty and Dana, I also noticed the rainbow colors of the ActivESSENCE sprays in giant 8 oz bottles. With so many scented body sprays on the market these days, it was tempting to ignore them, but ever the curious fragrance lover, I figured "What the heck, let's give them a try!" The first scent I tried was Moroccan Amber & Fig and I immediately recognized and liked the gourmand aroma. The question remained though, "Will this really last 12 hours?" Ironically, the following week, the company got in touch with me and offered to send me the collection for review purposes. I've now had them for three weeks and I'm ready to share my thoughts. Amazon.com offers the following brand description: "As a leading company in the fragrance arena, ACTIVESSENCE was specifically created to address two of the biggest consumer complaints of fragrance/body mists in the marketplace today: the scent dissipates quickly and leaves the skin feeling dry. Utilizing the newest in fragrance technology, ACTIVESSENCE time released fragrance mist provides a long wear fragrance experience plus skin-loving benefits. Infused with Vitamins A, C, D and E combined with natural antioxidants that promote healthier looking skin, ACTIVESSENCE delivers a premium, high quality fragrance experience that lasts up to 12 hours. An industry first that will be sure to revolutionize the fragrance category." The 4 fragrances with skin-loving extracts are advertised as follows ( InStyleActivessence.com ): "Our breakthrough technology provides “ Time Released ” micro encapsulates extending the wear of the fragrance. ActivEssence goes on soft and lasts all day!" "ActivEssence delivers an instant rush of moisture to your skin. ActivEssence contains essential oils, natural extracts and natural antioxidants-vitamins A, C, and E promoting healthy skin. ActivEssence delivers a high quality fragrance experience" ActivESSENCE is created by InStyle, a brand known for designer alternative fragrances in drug stores and markets throughout the USA. Renewal: Exotic Cherry Blossom Designer's Description: Exotic Cherry Blossom "Evokes the beauty of the exotic gardens of Japan with a graceful, award-winning blend of pear, Japanese Cherry Blossom & Sandalwood... Cherry Blossom and so much more, an exotic scent that draws attention!" Reminds me of: Zara Cherry , Michel Klein Comedie , YSL Young Sexy Lovely , BBW Japanese Cherry Blossom , and Marc Jacobs Lola . Marlen's thoughts: I was surprised to find an actual cherry aroma here, and I don't mean cherry flavor, but the smell of jarred fruit. The pear is quite subtle (though juicy) and the sandalwood (as I'd expected it) is nowhere to be found, though there is a definite woody note lingering at the base. Overall it is a nice aroma for those into the fruitier scents (but in a more grown up manner). As the fragrance lingered on my skin I was reminded of Cartier Delices . Enticing: Moroccan Orchid & Amber Designer's Description: Moroccan Orchid & Amber is a "Time Released Fragrance Mist Lemon Verbena, succulent Apricot, Desert Sand Accord, Spiced Fig, Creamy Vanilla, Kashmir Wood...One of our most popular fragrances, the Perfect Signature Fragrance!" Reminds me of: Without a doubt, this one smells like classic Thierry Mugler Angel. Marlen's thoughts: Within minutes of spraying Moroccan Orchid & Amber on my skin this morning while writing this article, a family member walked into my room asking, "Is that YOU that smells so amazing? Did you just spray something? It's WONDERFUL!" Well....how about that? I quite agree. I have always loved Angel so this was an instant win for me. I don't get fig or amber but this one was love at first sniff and the scent that I had initially tried in Walgreens that piqued my curiosity about the brand. The drydown on my skin went towards ginger bread...a little spicy! Relaxing: French Vanilla Lavender Designer's Description: French Vanilla Lavender is a "Skin-loving formula scented with a romantic bouquet of French Lavender, Nectarine de Provence & sun-kissed honey and a touch of White Chocolate ...Amazing fragrance that captures the senses…oh so sexy!" Reminds me of: The general vibe of French Vanilla Lavender reminds me of a sweeter, more vanillic Meteorites by Guerlain, Bvlgari's new Ashlemah or BBW French Lavender & Honey . Yes, these are three very different scents, but in some ways I get hints of all three. Marlen's thoughts: This one is curious as I don't really get lavender but heliotrope and violet...and lots of it. Where's the white chocolate? There is a hint of fruit hiding at the edges, perhaps the nectarine? Invigorating: Tropical Coconut Coast Designer's Description: Tropical Coconut Coast is "A Sunny blend of Coconut Blossom & Exotic Tiger Lilly, warmed by Molten Amber and tangy Verbena...WOW!...a tropical experience awaits, bathing suit optional!" Reminds me of: BBW Coconut Lime Verbena , Victoria's Secret Sparkling Citrus Lemon Escape , Jeanne Arthes Sexy Coconut , Mariella Burani Eau Rosee , Estee Lauder Bronze Goddess Eau Fraiche Skinscent . Marlen's thoughts: Definitely coconut and citrus! The coconut is surprisingly not too chalky, the way it often is in many aromas, and the citrus doesn't conjure thoughts of sugary lemon drops. I don't really get any lily and the green graphics and juice don't really match the vibe of the aroma. Definitely beachy and summery! The Bottom Line: The fragrances are all very pleasant and completely affordable with Moroccan Orchid & Amber being my standout favorite, especially as it develops. Though after about 3-4 hours the scents all become quiet, they do in fact linger on the skin for a surprisingly long while with Tropical Coconut Coast and Moroccan Orchid & Amber staying truest to their original aromas. I haven't used them often enough to comment on their skin-softening or moisturizing properties. ActiveESSENCE Fragrance Mists are priced at $7.99 USD for 8 fl. oz./236 ml and can be purchased online and in person at Walgreens , CVS, Target.com , Amazon , and Drugstore.com.
