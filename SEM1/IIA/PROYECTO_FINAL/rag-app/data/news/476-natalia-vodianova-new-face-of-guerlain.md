# Natalia Vodianova – New Face of Guerlain

Category: Art Books Events. Author: Sandra Raičević Petrović.

Russian Model, Natalia Vodianova, born in 1982 in Nizhna Novgorod, Russia, is one of refreshments by the house of Guerlain . She was chosen for one of the faces who will present old and new editions by this house. Something very special is just being prepared with her, but this is still kept a secret. Beautiful Natalia started the cooperation by advertising Shalimar for Guerlain in order to celebrate the 180th anniversary of the house with an aim to present new advertising campaigns of their legendary editions. A video advertisement has recently been published, as well as a printed advertising campaign. We can do nothing but enjoy Shalimar , as well as the new presentation. Source: elle.it , zimbio.com
